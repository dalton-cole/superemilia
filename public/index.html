How to play

Move with ← → or A / D. Hold Shift or Run for a running start. Hold your jump for extra height; jump again in the air. Land on little grumps to bounce off them. Hit glowing ? blocks from below for stars and power-ups. Some ordinary-looking openings hide coin games. Stand on one and press ↓ or S to explore. Earn hidden gems in bonus rooms, reach a checkpoint flag, and rescue the friend at the big finish flag. E uses your world power. P pauses. You always have unlimited tries.
